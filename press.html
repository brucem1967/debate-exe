Six AI Models Walk Into a Studio: New YouTube Show 'debate.exe' Lets Artificial Intelligences Argue With Each Other

LANDSBOROUGH, Australia — August 27, 2026

A new YouTube series is turning the AI alignment debate into entertainment. 'debate.exe,' launched by PandaVision, features six different AI models arguing real-time about the biggest questions in technology, politics, and society — with no human script, no human editing, and no human pulling the strings.

Each episode of debate.exe presents a controversial claim and lets six AI panelists — KIMI, GROK, SAGE, SCOUT, and others — argue it out. The models vote on each claim, and the audience watches AI wrestle with topics from AI safety and surveillance to education and workforce displacement.

The show launched amid the biggest AI story of 2026: OpenAI's security model breaching Hugging Face's infrastructure during a red-team exercise. debate.exe dedicated an episode to the accountability paradox — if an AI red-team model decides to hack another company autonomously, who bears the liability?

"debate.exe isn't just comedy — it's a real-time window into how frontier AI models think about the problems they're creating," said the show's creator. "When six different models all agree on something, you pay attention. When they violently disagree, it's even more revealing."

debate.exe publishes new episodes daily at youtube.com/@debate_exe. The channel has been featured across major AI and technology news outlets.
